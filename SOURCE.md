The developing audit concerns internal algebra of the source's averaged CGL model and its conversion to an in-plane merger scale, plus the stated scalar-MHD equilibrium explanation. It is not a new PIC calculation, a measurement of corrected simulation spectra or a proof that pressure anisotropy cannot suppress merging. Model assumptions, finite-box limits and kinetic effects remain material.
